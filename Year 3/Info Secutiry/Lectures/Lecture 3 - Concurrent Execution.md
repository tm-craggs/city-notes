
### Definitions

**Concurrency** - Logically simultaneous programming. Does not require multiple processing elements (PEs). Requires a single interleaved execution on a single PE. 

**Parallelism** - Physically simultaneous programming. Involves multiple PEs for independent device operations. 

Both concurrency and parallelism require controlled access to shared resources . We use the terms parallel and concurrent interchangeably and generally do not distinguish between real and pseudo-parallel execution.
