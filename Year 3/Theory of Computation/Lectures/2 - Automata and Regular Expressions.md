## Regular Expressions

- **Regular languages** comprise the class of all languages recognisable by Finite State Automata
- Regular languages may also be described by **regular expressions**
- Definition: The class of REs for a given alphabet $\Sigma$ is defined by
	- Every letter of $\Sigma$ is a RE
	- $\varepsilon$ 
	- $\emptyset$ is a regular expression
	- If r and s are RE then so are: (r), rs, r $\cup$ s, r*
	- Nothing else is an RE
